# Syllentra AI

## Project vision

### Problem statement

Students often face a large amount of educational content spread across lecture recordings, slides, PDFs, notes, and other materials. These sources are frequently fragmented, dense, and difficult to navigate effectively. Learners may struggle to identify the most important concepts, connect ideas across sources, and apply explanations in a way that matches their current understanding.

### Target users

This project is intended for students and learners who want a more adaptive and structured educational experience, especially in academic or technical contexts where concepts build cumulatively over time.

### Objectives

The project aims to:

- Reduce friction in understanding educational materials
- Support learners with contextual explanations and personalized tutoring
- Connect information across documents, recordings, and conceptual summaries
- Encourage active learning through questioning, practice, and feedback
- Offer flexible learning strategies that adapt to the task and learner needs
- Promote reliable, transparent, and privacy-conscious educational assistance

### Expected capabilities

The long-term vision includes:

1. Document understanding for PDFs, slides, notes, and additional educational materials
2. Audio understanding for lecture recordings and transcripts
3. Adaptive explanations based on learner profile and demonstrated progress
4. Context-aware learning strategy selection such as page-by-page, concept-by-concept, or overview-based guidance
5. Verification and supplementation of educational claims using trusted sources
6. Generation of exercises, quizzes, and worked examples
7. Support for continuity of learning over time when authorized
8. Multilingual support for French, German, and English
9. Architecture extensibility to support future tools, models, and specialized agents

### Design principles

- Research and document decisions before implementation
- Prefer modular and maintainable design
- Keep security, privacy, and consent central to the architecture
- Favor incremental development with verifiable milestones
- Avoid premature technology lock-in before evaluation
- Do not claim planned functionality as implemented
- Keep the learning experience understandable, respectful, and accessible

### Non-goals for the initial phase

This project does not assume a final stack, product readiness, or deployment model. The initial objective is to define the problem clearly, document requirements, and establish an architecture that can support future implementation responsibly.
